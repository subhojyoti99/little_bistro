we create connections.

Located on Karaya Road, The Little Bistro Lounge is your neighborhood sanctuary. Whether you're here for a quick morning brew, a casual brunch with friends, or a late-night shisha session, we provide an atmosphere that feels like a warm embrace.

As a women-owned establishment, we pride ourselves on being an inclusive, family-friendly space where every dish is made with love and every corner invites you to stay just a little longer.
